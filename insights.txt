
KEY INSIGHTS
1. Overall average marks: 69.4/100 | Pass rate: 97.3% (8 students failing).
2. Strongest subject: Urdu (73.0). Weakest subject: Math (64.5).
3. Study hours have a strong positive correlation with marks (r = 0.82). Students studying 5+ hrs/day average
   82.5 vs 55.9 for those studying under 2 hrs.
4. Attendance also matters (r = 0.35). Students with >90% attendance average 73.0
   vs 63.9 for those below 75%.
5. Class A performs best and Class C performs lowest.

RECOMMENDATIONS
- Run extra remedial/practice sessions for Math.
- Set a minimum 75% attendance policy and follow up early with low-attendance students.
- Encourage structured study of at least 4-5 hours/day; provide study guides and timetables.
- Give Class C additional teacher support and peer-learning with Class A.
- Identify at-risk students (low study hours + low attendance) early and provide mentoring.
